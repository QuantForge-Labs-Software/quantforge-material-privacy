QuantForge Material Calculator privacy policy

QuantForge Labs · 25 November 2026

The calculator processes measurements, prices, currency codes and estimation results locally on your Windows device. QuantForge Labs does not receive these entries, identify users, or collect analytics through the application.

Drafts and reports

Drafts stay in memory during your session. Closing the application discards them. When you export an estimate, Windows lets you choose a file location. The resulting text report contains your measurements, allowance, rate and calculation results. You can remove it using your device’s file manager. If you choose a shared or synchronized folder, your storage provider may copy that file according to your settings.

Store purchases

Microsoft handles purchases and distribution through the Microsoft Store under its own privacy terms. The calculator does not request payment details or an account.

Visiting this policy page

GitHub Pages hosts this page when it is published there. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. Reading the policy does not send calculator entries to GitHub.
